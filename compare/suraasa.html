thegurucool vs Suraasa: which fits your growth?

Both platforms help teachers in Asia grow in their careers. One is built around formal qualifications, the other around AI competency and coaching. Here is a plain comparison so you can choose.

The short answer

Suraasa is a teacher education and career platform anchored in India, best known for its accredited iPGCE qualification, live masterclasses and the CPAT benchmark. It is a paid, cohort-led path. thegurucool measures a teacher's AI competency across seven domains, then the Guru, a personal AI coach, works with them on real tasks from their own classroom. Teachers can start free on their own, and schools can roll it out to whole staff teams.

What is Suraasa good at?

Suraasa gives teachers a formal, recognised qualification, which most PD platforms cannot. If you plan to teach internationally or move into a role that expects a postgraduate teaching credential, the iPGCE is a serious option. Its live masterclasses and cohort structure also suit teachers who learn best with set dates and a group around them.

For teachers near Mumbai, the in-person Teacher Development Centres add a physical touchpoint that online-only platforms do not have.

How is thegurucool different?

thegurucool starts with a narrower, practical question: how ready is this teacher to teach well with AI, and where should they grow first? Each teacher takes a scenario-based diagnostic across seven domains, from Technology Fluency to Impact Amplification, and gets a personalised pathway.

The learning happens with the Guru, a personal AI coach. Each lesson is a short conversation built around one real task from the teacher's own subject and classroom. The Guru asks questions, models approaches and pushes for specifics, then the teacher leaves with a next step to try in class. Lessons are grounded in frameworks educators already trust, including Bloom's Taxonomy and TPACK.

There is no cohort to wait for and no fee for individual teachers. For schools, the result is data on which teaching practices are changing across staff.

Which one should you choose?

Suraasa may suit you if

  • You want an accredited postgraduate teaching qualification
  • You are planning a move into international teaching
  • You prefer a cohort with set dates and live sessions
  • You are based in India, especially near Mumbai

thegurucool may suit you if

  • You want to start on your own, today, for free
  • You need to build AI competency in your own classroom
  • You teach in Southeast Asia or India and want local context
  • Your school wants to see how practice is changing across staff

The two can also work side by side. A teacher might study for a qualification with Suraasa and use thegurucool to build AI competency along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Is thegurucool an alternative to Suraasa's iPGCE?

Not directly. Suraasa's iPGCE is an accredited postgraduate teaching qualification run over about 12 months. thegurucool does not offer a degree-level qualification. It measures AI competency across seven domains and coaches teachers on real tasks from their own classroom, and teachers can start free.

Can I use Suraasa and thegurucool together?

Yes. Many teachers will. Suraasa covers formal qualifications and broad teaching practice. thegurucool focuses on teaching well with AI, which most teacher qualifications do not cover in depth.

Which is better for teachers in Southeast Asia?

Suraasa is anchored in India, with its Teacher Development Centres in Mumbai. thegurucool is built in Singapore for teachers and educators across Southeast Asia and India, with local classroom context in its coaching.

How much does thegurucool cost?

thegurucool is free for individual teachers. Schools can contact admin@thegurucool.ai for pricing.
