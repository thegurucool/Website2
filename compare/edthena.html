thegurucool vs Edthena: which fits your teachers?

Both platforms use AI to help teachers grow. They do it in very different ways, and they are open to different people. Here is a plain comparison so you can choose.

The short answer

Edthena is a video coaching platform. Teachers record their lessons, and AI helps them reflect on what happened. It is sold to schools and districts. thegurucool measures a teacher's AI competency across seven domains, then the Guru, a personal AI coach, works with them on a real task from their own classroom. Teachers can start free on their own, and schools can roll it out to whole staff teams.

What is Edthena good at?

Edthena is a well-established way to coach teachers with their own classroom video. Watching yourself teach is one of the most honest forms of feedback there is, and Edthena makes that process structured and easy to share with a coach. Its AI coaching work was recognised in TIME's Best Inventions list in 2025.

If your school already uses Edthena and your teachers are comfortable filming lessons, it is a strong tool for reflecting on general practice.

How is thegurucool different?

thegurucool starts with a different question: how ready is this teacher to teach well with AI, and where should they grow first? Each teacher takes a scenario-based diagnostic across seven domains, from Technology Fluency to Impact Amplification, and gets a personalised pathway.

The learning happens with the Guru, a personal AI coach. Each lesson is a short conversation built around one real task from the teacher's own subject and classroom. The Guru asks questions, models approaches and pushes for specifics, then the teacher leaves with a next step to try in class. Lessons are grounded in frameworks educators already trust, including Bloom's Taxonomy and TPACK.

For schools, the result is data on which teaching practices are changing, not a list of who attended a workshop.

Which one should you choose?

Edthena may suit you if

  • Your school or district already has a licence
  • You want feedback on general teaching through lesson video
  • Filming lessons is practical and accepted in your school

thegurucool may suit you if

  • You want to start on your own, today, for free
  • You need to build AI competency, not only general practice
  • You teach in Southeast Asia or India and want local context
  • Your school wants to see how practice is changing across staff

The two can also work side by side. A school might use Edthena for lesson observation and thegurucool to build AI competency across its teaching staff.

Frequently asked questions

Can an individual teacher sign up for Edthena?

Edthena is sold to schools and districts, so most teachers get access through their employer. thegurucool is free for individual teachers to sign up and start on their own.

Is thegurucool a video coaching platform like Edthena?

No. Edthena coaches teachers by analysing recordings of their lessons. thegurucool starts with a scenario-based diagnostic across seven domains, then coaches each teacher through short lessons with the Guru, built around a real task from their own classroom.

Can a school use Edthena and thegurucool together?

Yes. They answer different questions. Edthena helps teachers reflect on what happened in a recorded lesson. thegurucool measures AI competency across seven domains and coaches teachers on how to teach with AI.

How much does thegurucool cost?

thegurucool is free for individual teachers. Schools can contact admin@thegurucool.ai for pricing.
