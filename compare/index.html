AI teacher PD platforms, compared.

Ten platforms that help teachers grow with AI, side by side. We keep this page updated, so you can see what each one does well and who it suits.

The short answer

Most AI teacher PD falls into a few groups. Tool platforms like MagicSchool build fluency with their own AI tools. Literacy trainers like AI for Education run workshops and courses. Coaching and observation platforms like Edthena, BetterLesson and Teachstone are usually bought by schools. Suraasa offers accredited qualifications anchored in India. thegurucool starts with a competency diagnostic across seven domains, then coaches each teacher on real tasks from their own classroom, and individual teachers can start free.

What does each platform do well?

thegurucool

A scenario-based diagnostic across the seven TEACH-AI domains, a personalised pathway, and short coaching lessons with the Guru built around a real task from your own classroom. Schools see which teaching practices are changing.

Best for: teachers and schools in Southeast Asia and India building AI competency.

Suraasa

An India-anchored teacher education and career platform. Suraasa Plus bundles the CPAT benchmark, a self-paced course, live masterclasses and AI tools. Its accredited qualifications are a real strength. See thegurucool vs Suraasa.

Best for: teachers who want a formal postgraduate teaching qualification.

MagicSchool

A widely used AI tool suite for teachers, with lesson planners, quiz builders and more. Its free certification courses teach you to use those tools well.

Best for: teachers who want quick, practical fluency with AI tools.

AI for Education

AI literacy training through workshops, courses and a large free resource library, with a strong voice on AI policy in education.

Best for: schools that want a consistent AI literacy baseline across staff.

Colleague.ai

An AI assistant for teachers, linked to the AICE badge from the University of Washington. Badge evidence comes from how teachers use the Colleague.ai platform.

Best for: US schools already using Colleague.ai.

Edthena

Video-based coaching. Teachers record lessons and reflect on them with AI prompts and coach feedback. See thegurucool vs Edthena.

Best for: schools that already license it and are comfortable filming lessons.

ISTE and ASCD

Well-produced courses and certifications tied to the ISTE Standards, run on set schedules and paid per course.

Best for: teachers who want a widely recognised US credential.

BetterLesson

Coaching delivered by human instructional coaches, set up through schools and districts.

Best for: districts that want human coaching across staff.

Teachstone

Home of the CLASS framework, a well-researched way to observe and improve teacher and student interactions. Training is often in person.

Best for: schools and early years settings using CLASS observation.

PowerSchool

A student information system company. Its Coaching and Mentoring product adds educator growth tracking inside the wider PowerSchool suite.

Best for: districts already running PowerSchool.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI professional development platform for teachers in Southeast Asia in 2026?

It depends on what you need. For a competency diagnostic, a personalised pathway and a coach that works on your own classroom, thegurucool is built for teachers in Southeast Asia and India and is free for individual teachers. For an accredited postgraduate qualification, Suraasa is a strong option. For fast fluency with AI tools, MagicSchool offers free certification courses.

How is thegurucool different from Suraasa?

Suraasa is a teacher education and career platform anchored in India, best known for accredited qualifications, live masterclasses and a CPAT benchmark. thegurucool focuses on AI competency. It runs a scenario-based diagnostic across seven TEACH-AI domains, builds a personalised pathway, and coaches each teacher through short lessons built around their own classroom.

How is thegurucool different from Colleague.ai?

Colleague.ai is an AI assistant for teachers, linked to the AICE badge from the University of Washington, with evidence drawn from how teachers use the Colleague.ai platform. thegurucool is a coach rather than an assistant. Teachers take a diagnostic across seven domains and the Guru coaches them on real tasks from their own classroom, with local context for Southeast Asia and India.

Is MagicSchool a replacement for teacher professional development?

MagicSchool is a strong AI tool suite with free certification courses that build tool fluency well. It does not run a competency diagnostic across teaching practice or build a pathway around a teacher's subject and classroom. Many teachers use a tool suite like MagicSchool alongside a coaching platform.

Which competency frameworks do these platforms use?

thegurucool's TEACH-AI framework draws on Bloom's Taxonomy, TPACK, Universal Design for Learning and Hattie's Visible Learning, applied to AI-integrated classrooms across seven domains. Colleague.ai uses the AICE framework from the University of Washington. ISTE credentials tie to the ISTE Standards. Teachstone uses its CLASS observation framework. MagicSchool and AI for Education focus on AI literacy and tool use.

Can an individual teacher use these platforms without school approval?

Yes for thegurucool, MagicSchool, ISTE and Suraasa. Partly for AI for Education and Colleague.ai, which offer some free individual access but deliver much of their work through schools and districts. Mostly no for Edthena, BetterLesson, Teachstone and PowerSchool, which are usually bought by schools or districts.
